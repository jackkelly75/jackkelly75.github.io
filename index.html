12-week learning plan

Pimsleur trains your ear and speaking. Hello Chinese adds reading, characters and vocabulary. The focus sessions fill the gaps neither covers well: tones, writing, grammar, reading and talking to a real person. After week 12, repeat the cycle with HSK 2 material.

Weeks 1–4: Sounds and survival
  • Goal: read pinyin aloud with the right tones, and handle greetings, numbers and ordering food.
  • Pimsleur: aim to finish roughly Level 1 over this phase, repeating any lesson where you froze.
  • Check at week 4: say 20 phrases from memory without prompts.
Weeks 5–8: Characters and sentences
  • Goal: recognise about 100 common characters and build simple sentences.
  • Start a flashcard deck (Anki) for every new word, with the pinyin and a sentence.
  • Check at week 8: write 10 simple sentences about yourself from memory.
Weeks 9–12: Reading and conversation
  • Goal: read short graded texts and hold a basic 5-minute conversation.
  • Book a tutor or language partner for at least one session per week.
  • Check at week 12: a 5-minute chat with a tutor, plus an HSK 1 practice test.

Resources

  • Pimsleur: audio course for listening and speaking. Say every answer out loud.
  • Hello Chinese: lessons, writing practice and HSK-aligned courses.
  • Pleco: the standard dictionary app, with handwriting lookup.
  • Anki: spaced-repetition flashcards. Skritter is a paid alternative for writing characters.
  • Chinese Grammar Wiki: free grammar explanations sorted by level.
  • Du Chinese: graded reading with audio, from beginner level.
  • italki and HelloTalk: paid tutors and free language exchange partners.
